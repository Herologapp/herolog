2. The short version

Herolog stores all of your game data only on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking, and no data is sent to us or to third parties.

3. What data does the app process?

The app stores the following locally on your device: your quests and when you completed them, your progress (level, experience, gold, gems, streak), your hero (name, appearance, equipment), your adventure progress and your settings (e.g. music, sound, vibration). This data never leaves your device. We have no access to it.

You can choose any name for your hero. Please do not enter sensitive information.

4. Sharing and exporting

When you use “Share hero”, the app creates an image on your device. It is only passed on if you send it to another app yourself using your device’s share dialog. That app’s privacy policy then applies. The same goes for “Export progress”: the file is only saved on your device.

5. Permissions

The app uses your device’s vibration for short feedback (can be turned off under “More”). It does not need access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

6. Google Play

When you download the app from Google Play, Google processes data (e.g. your Google account and device information) under its own responsibility. Google’s privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep it secure (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The font is loaded from Google Fonts, which transmits your IP address to Google. We do not use cookies or analytics tools.

8. Your rights

Since we do not store any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose. You can delete your data in the app at any time: via “More → Reset progress” or by uninstalling the app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

9. Changes

If the app gets new features that process data in the future (e.g. advertising, purchases or online features), we will update this policy beforehand and let you know in the app.
